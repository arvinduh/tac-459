This AI-powered career advisor provides personalized job recommendations based
on skill analysis and real-time job market insights. It integrates with Bing and
LinkedIn APIs for real-time job search.

## How it was built

The system uses GPT-4o for skill matching, cosine similarity, and real-time job
updates, ensuring accurate and up-to-date recommendations.
