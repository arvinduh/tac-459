HireWizard is an AI-driven job search assistant that matches job seekers to
relevant roles based on resume analysis and job posting data.

## How it was built

It uses NLP-powered resume screening, cosine similarity for job matching, and
integrates with job databases like LinkedIn and Indeed to improve hiring
outcomes.
