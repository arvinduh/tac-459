This AI-powered tool transcribes, summarizes, and allows users to interact with
video content through a chatbot. It extracts key insights from lectures,
meetings, and online videos.

## How it was built

The system uses LLM-based summarization, video transcription, and chatbot
features to provide interactive question-answering based on video content.
