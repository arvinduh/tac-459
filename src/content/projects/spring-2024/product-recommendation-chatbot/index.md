The Product Recommendation Chatbot simplifies consumer tech purchases by
delivering personalized product recommendations, explanations, and comparisons,
reducing decision-making complexity and enhancing user shopping experience
across multiple electronic categories.

## How it was built

Collect product data via APIs, deploy cosine similarity algorithms for product
matching, integrate GPT-generated recommendation explanations, monetize through
subscriptions and affiliate partnerships, and expand product categories
regularly.
