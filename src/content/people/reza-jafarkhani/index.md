Reza Jafarkhani is a Senior Lecturer of Technology and Applied Computing at the
USC Viterbi School of Engineering, specializing in machine learning, artificial
intelligence, and data analytics. He teaches all courses in the USC AI Minor,
helping students build AI capabilities from foundational concepts to advanced
applications. With extensive experience teaching students across the program, he
connects technical theory with practical implementation and guides learners as
they develop their AI skills. He co-instructs TAC 459 with Professor Allen
Bolourchi, bringing this broad teaching experience to the course's technical
foundations and hands-on AI product development.

His academic and industry background informs a practical approach to teaching
AI, machine learning, and programming. He emphasizes connecting technical
concepts with real-world applications and preparing students to apply their
skills to engineering and industry problems.
