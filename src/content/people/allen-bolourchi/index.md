Allen Bolourchi leads AI research and product at **Meta Superintelligence Labs**
(MSL), Fundamental AI Research (FAIR), and is an Adjunct Professor of Generative
AI and NLP at the USC Viterbi School of Engineering, where he directs the
capstone of the USC AI Minor (TAC 459), which he designed from the ground up. At
Meta, his work spans frontier models, agentic AI, world models, computer-use
agents, and multimodal intelligence. He also co-leads the development of Meta's
open-weight model Muse Glimmer and has co-authored research with Yann LeCun and
other FAIR researchers. Previously, he led AI/ML personalization for the App
Store and Apple Arcade at Apple.

His teaching combines the technical foundations of generative AI and NLP with AI
product strategy and hands-on development. The capstone brings together
foundational concepts, analysis of AI businesses, and the design and
demonstration of working AI products.

His research background includes predictive modeling, evolutionary optimization,
and the analysis of complex systems. He has also worked as a machine learning
research scientist at Caltech.
